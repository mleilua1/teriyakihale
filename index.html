About Teriyaki Hale

Hale Means House or Home

Aloha Means Welcome to Our House

Teriyaki Hale brings bold, welcoming island flavors to the heart of Utah. What started as a family-run festival food booth has grown into a full island catering experience built for fairs, corporate events, church gatherings, weddings, and private celebrations.

We specialize in flavorful teriyaki chicken, thinly sliced beef, and Kalua pork served with our signature sides and house-made sauces. Our famous Hale Lava Sauce — a creamy, spicy blend inspired by island heat — has become a crowd favorite at festivals and events alike.

For larger gatherings, our Island Catering Packages bring the full Hale experience to you — including premium selections like Teriyaki Garlic Shrimp and Hale Poke for select events.

Whether you’re visiting us at a festival or booking us for your next celebration, Teriyaki Hale is about more than food — it’s about hospitality, community, and sharing island flavor in every bite.

Find us across Salt Lake and Utah County — or book us for your next event.
